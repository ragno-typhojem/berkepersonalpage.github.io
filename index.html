OPEN SOURCEGAMEPLAY SYSTEMS

Advanced R15
Weapon Framework

A tool-based, extensible Roblox weapon system. Character motion, weapon handling and environmental interaction come together through code-driven animation.

  • Luau
  • CFrame / Motor6D / Tween
  • IK
  • FPS / TPS
Inside the system
  • R15 rig integration with IK-based arm positioning and procedural character animation.
  • Weapon sway, inertia and recoil, with firing, reloads and weapon-specific behaviour.
  • FPS and optional TPS support, environmental interaction and modular weapon configurations.

CUSTOM PHYSICS · CHARACTER CONTROL

Euphoria

Exploring a custom Roblox parkour and physics system, with manually controlled movement, gravity and animation.

  • Luau
  • Raycasting
  • Mantling
  • Roll-up
Project notes

Development focuses on wall detection, character-aware raycast lengths, jumping, running and climbing, with mantling and roll-up mechanics under development.

ARCADE FOOTBALL · MULTIPLAYER

KICKVANE

Arcade football built around a readable playfield, directional shooting and the relationship between input, simulation and feedback.

  • Luau
  • Client–server
  • Responsive UI
Project notes

Work includes modular client–server gameplay, configurable ball physics, aiming indicators and interface design for keyboard, touch and controller input.

TEAM PLAY · GAME INTERFACES

Hustle Rush

A multiplayer Roblox project pairing competitive team mechanics with a vibrant, simulation-inspired interface.

  • Luau
  • UI / UX
  • Modular systems
Project notes

Focus areas include runtime GUI generation, responsive menus and HUDs, clear role selection and maintainable game modules.
